Cashdeck

Insights redesign

Round 2. Home shows direction, not just numbers: every card answers “better or worse than last month, and what is already committed?” with one big number, one comparison and one small chart. Dark (OLED) is the hero; light keeps the same hierarchy. Extends Foundation: every existing token name and component is kept; changes are listed in 01.
